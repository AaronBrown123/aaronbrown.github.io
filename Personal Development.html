Twelve Pillars


Chapters 4- 7 of “Twelve Pillars” by Jim Rohn and Chris Widener (pp.43–80) can be refered to as a giude to success. Four major keys for success were refered to throughout these chapteres these include setting goals , effectivly using you time by prioritizing tasks , being mindful of you sourroundings and the persons you associate with and being open to learning new things.

   Setting goals is a good way to determine where you and where you want to be and also how youre going to get there, there are mainly three types of goals i.e short term goals , mid term goals and long term goals. Setting goals could help me to decide on the things i would like to accomplish and how long it would take me to do so , it also give me a sense of direction. short term goals can be used to develop myself as in individual for example a short term goal could be to complete a book over the course of a month which could have possitive effects such as developing my vocabulary as well as increase my knowledge.

   Prioritizing what activities i want to complete can help to give me a sense of focus for example organizing my assignments based on due dates can help to reduces the chances of me overlooking an assignment.

    Keeping track of your environment can help you to identify things or persons that may negatively impact you for example surrounding yourself with persons who love to procrastinate will lead to you being a procrastinator which is why one must observe the persons they are around and determine whether or not they would like to associate themselves with.

   Being open to learn new things allows for growth within an individual the moment a person decides to stop learning there isnt any room for growth.
